These PNG files are the unchanged embedded emoji resources of the locally
installed Windows Weixin 4.1.15.51. Label mappings come from the same client.
They are used solely to render WeChat face labels faithfully in local previews
and offline chat archives. The project's MIT license does not cover this artwork.

Extraction: scripts/extract_wechat_native_faces.py
provenance.json records the source DLL and individual PNG SHA-256 hashes.
